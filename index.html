Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Powerful features that put you in control

Encrypted multi-admin vault

Keys, configs, and credentials live in a dual-layer AES-256-GCM + ChaCha20-Poly1305 vault with Argon2id and TOTP 2FA. Steal the file without the passphrase and you get nothing.

Tamper-evident audit log

Every privileged action is SHA-256 hash-chained on your own machine. Alter or delete an entry and WireSeal flags it. Your logs stay with you — not in someone else's cloud.

Per-client access control

Restrict each client to specific LAN resources — e.g. only Plex on 10.0.0.10:32400 — enforced by the host firewall. Least privilege by default, not all-or-nothing.

Access anything, from anywhere

An encrypted WireGuard tunnel from any network — SSH terminals, SFTP, NAS, IP cameras, smart home — with live per-peer connection stats and LAN device discovery.

Encrypted, scheduled backups

Back up your vault to local disk, SSH, or WebDAV on a schedule, and restore safely. The backup is the encrypted vault itself — useless to anyone without your passphrase.

Zero telemetry, fully yours

No cloud control plane, no account, no analytics. Open source and security-audited; the management API binds to localhost only. Verify the code — and the audit — yourself.
