Encryption

Your keys. Your vault. Your rules.

WireSeal's vault uses dual-layer encryption — AES-256-GCM on the outside, ChaCha20-Poly1305 on the inside. Your passphrase is stretched with Argon2id, the memory-hard KDF that makes brute-force attacks impractical.

  • AES-256-GCM outer encryption layer
  • ChaCha20-Poly1305 inner encryption layer
  • Argon2id key derivation (memory-hard)
  • TOTP 2FA for admin access
  • 256-bit secrets, 8 backup codes

Audit Trail

Every action. Hash-chained. Tamper-proof.

WireSeal logs every privileged action to a local audit file. Each entry includes a SHA-256 hash of the previous entry, creating a blockchain-like chain. Modify any record and the chain breaks — WireSeal detects it instantly.

  • SHA-256 hash chain for integrity
  • Timestamps, actor identity, action details
  • Tamper detection on every read
  • Exportable for compliance
  • Stored locally — never leaves your machine
How it works

Up and running in ten minutes.

One command to set up the server, one port-forward on your router, and you're running an audited, self-hosted gateway.

1

Initialize

Run wireseal init — generates server keys, creates vault, configures firewall, starts WireGuard. One command does it all.

2

Add clients

Run wireseal add-client alice — generates client keys, stores in vault, outputs QR code. Scan with your phone. Done.

3

Connect

Open the WireGuard app, scan the QR code, and you're on your home network from anywhere. Access SSH, NAS, cameras, everything.
