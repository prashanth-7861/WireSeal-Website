Capabilities

A gateway, not a configurator.

Everything you need to run secure, controlled, observable access to your home — shipped in v1.0.0.

Encrypted multi-admin vault

Keys, configs, and credentials live in a dual-layer AES-256-GCM + ChaCha20-Poly1305 vault with Argon2id and TOTP 2FA. Steal the file without the passphrase and you get nothing.

Tamper-evident audit log

Every privileged action is SHA-256 hash-chained on your own machine. Alter or delete an entry and WireSeal flags it. Your logs stay with you — not in someone else's cloud.

Per-client ACLs

Restrict each client to specific LAN resources — enforced by the host firewall.

Access from anywhere

SSH, SFTP, NAS, cameras, smart home — with live per-peer stats and LAN discovery.

Scheduled backups

Encrypted vault backups to local, SSH, or WebDAV — on a schedule, restorable safely.

Zero telemetry, fully yours

No cloud control plane, no account, no analytics. Open source and security-audited; the management API binds to localhost only. Verify the code — and the audit — yourself.

The wedge

You can't beat a managed mesh at convenience. So WireSeal beats it where it counts: you own the keys, nothing phones home, and every action is provable.
