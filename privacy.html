Privacy & Security

Zero plaintext secrets. Local-first architecture. Your data stays yours.

Core Principles

  • Encrypted vault

    Your WireGuard keys never exist in plaintext. The vault is sealed with your passphrase, derived via Argon2id.

  • No account required

    No email, no signup, no cloud account. You are your own identity. Your server, your VPN.

  • Local-first

    All data stays on your server. No cloud sync, no external API calls. Works when internet is down.

  • You control your data

    Export your keys anytime. Backup to a password-protected file. Restore on any machine.

Encryption Details

Vault Encryption

AES-256-GCM with Argon2id key derivation. Your passphrase never stored. Only the verification hash.

VPN Protocol

WireGuard with ChaCha20-Poly1305. Post-quantum ready. Same protocol the NSA and EFF recommend.

Key Rotation

Rotate client keys anytime via CLI. Old keys revoked automatically. Or schedule automated rotation.

Two-Factor Auth

TOTP support via authenticator apps. Second factor required after PIN changes or device resets.

Frequently Asked

Can I lose access to my VPN if I forget my passphrase?

Yes — there's no "forgot password" flow because there's no account. That's by design. Your passphrase encrypts the vault. Without it, keys are unrecoverable. We recommend backing up your vault to a file.

What happens if my server is compromised?

An attacker would need your passphrase to decrypt the vault. Without it, your keys are safe. You can rotate server keys and revoke all clients instantly.

Can WireSeal see my keys?

No. The vault is sealed with your passphrase. Keys exist only in memory briefly during operations and are never logged or transmitted.

Is this open source?

Yes. WireSeal is open source on GitHub. You're free to audit the code, fork it, or run it yourself.
